Job Description

Remote, India · 4 to 5 Years of Experience

Department: Product Engineering

Full Time 5 Position Available.

  • Create and edit video content for multi-platform use and distribution for social media channels (Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, Snapchat, IGTV, Facebook Stories and Instagram Stories).
  • Design and create highly engaging industry-related content in both photo, gif & video format.
  • Publish posts on various social media channels.
  • Promote content on social networks and monitor engagement (e.g. comments and shares).
  • Research industry-related topics.
  • Editing audio and sound design on projects.
  • Engage in opportunities to develop original content and concepts for web and mobile.
  • Create motion graphics and animations using 2D and 3D applications for marketing and promotional usage.
  • Manage the day-to-day handling of all social media channels such as LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube, adapting content to suit different channels.
  • At least 4 years of professional experience building full-stack web applications.
  • Strong knowledge of JavaScript, modern frontend frameworks, and server-side development.
  • Experience designing and integrating REST APIs and working with relational databases.
  • Care about accessible, reliable, well-tested, and maintainable software.
  • Communicate clearly and work collaboratively with designers and product teams.
  • Design, build, and maintain responsive web applications from frontend to backend.
  • Work with the team to turn product requirements into clear technical solutions.
  • Write readable code, review contributions, and help maintain quality through testing.
  • Find and resolve performance, security, and reliability issues.
  • Share progress with teammates and contribute to continuous product improvements.
