Privacy Policy

Last Updated on 27th January 2021

Finsweet respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what information may be collected when you visit our website or contact our team, and how that information is used.

When you get in touch, you may choose to provide details such as your name, email address, telephone number, company, and the contents of your message. We use these details to respond to your enquiry and provide the information or services you request.

Basic technical information, such as your browser type and the pages you visit, may be available to us through the operation of the website. We use information of this kind to maintain and improve the site and to understand how visitors use it.

We only use personal information for the purposes described in this policy, or for another purpose that we explain when we collect it. We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information through this website.

We disclose your information in the following manner:

Service providers. Information may be shared with trusted suppliers who help us operate our website or respond to enquiries. They may use it only to provide services to us and must handle it appropriately.

Legal requirements. We may disclose information when required by law, regulation, or a valid legal process, or when disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, safety, and security of our visitors and business.

Business changes. If our business is reorganized or transferred, information may be included as part of that transaction. Where required, we will provide notice and explain any choices available to you.

How we protect and retain information

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Information is kept only for as long as it is needed to respond to you, provide a requested service, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Your choices and questions

You may contact us to ask about personal information you have provided, request a correction, or ask us to stop using it where applicable. To make a request or ask a question about this policy, email hello@finsweet.com.
